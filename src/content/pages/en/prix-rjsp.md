title: Sciences Po Law Review Award for Outstanding Legal Article
description: The annual award for the best legal article, open exclusively to Sciences Po students.
---

The author of the best submission each year, exclusively open to Sciences Po students, will be honored with the Sciences Po Law Review Award for Outstanding Legal Article.

Students may write on the legal topic of their choice in either English or French.

The winning contribution will receive a prize of 200 euros. Submissions will be evaluated based on quality of writing, originality, and contribution to legal scholarship.

For details on formatting, length, submission process, and other requirements, please refer to our [general blog article guidelines](/en/critères-de-soumission). Please submit your article through the [Google Forms link](https://forms.gle/VJdpN5ugG8V3U4Tp9).

**Articles should be sent by May 31, 2026 at 23:59.**
